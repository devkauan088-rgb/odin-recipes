Feijoada

About:

Feijoada or feijoada à brasileira (lit. Portuguese for "Brazilian-style feijoada") is a dish that consists of a stew of black beans with various types of pork and beef. It is served with farofa, white rice, sautéed collard green, and sliced oranges, among other sides. It is a popular dish, typical of Brazilian cuisine.

Ingredients

You do not need a whole lot of ingredients to make a darn good feijoada. A few pantry staples combined with a few cuts of pork and beef come together to make the most delicious black bean stew you’ve ever tasted!

  • Beans

    500 g (1.1 lb) dried black beans

  • Pork Sausage

    300 g (10.5 oz) pork sausage, sliced

  • Smoked Sausage

    300 g (10.5 oz) smoked sausage, sliced

  • Pork Ribs / Pork Shoulder

    300 g (10.5 oz) pork ribs or pork shoulder, cut into pieces

  • Bacon

    150 g (5 oz) bacon, chopped

  • Onion

    1 large onion, chopped

  • Garlic

    4 cloves of garlic, minced

  • Bay leaves

    2 bay leaves

  • Salt and Pepper

    Salt and pepper to taste

  • Oil

    1-2 tablespoons of oil

How to make it

  1. Soak the beans

    Place the black beans in a large bowl, cover them with plenty of water, and let them soak for 8–12 hours. Drain and rinse before cooking.

  2. Cook the beans

    Put the beans in a large pot and cover them with fresh water. Add the bay leaves and cook until the beans begin to become tender.

  3. Prepare the meat

    In another large pan, fry the bacon until it releases some of its fat. Add the sausages and pork pieces and cook until they are nicely browned.

  4. Add the aromatics

    Add the chopped onion and garlic to the meat. Cook for a few minutes until the onion becomes soft and fragrant.

  5. Combine everything

    Add the meat mixture to the pot with the beans. Add enough water to keep everything covered.

  6. Simmer

    Cook over low heat for about 1½–2 hours, stirring occasionally, until the beans are creamy and the meat is tender. Add more water if necessary.

  7. Reason

    Remove the bay leaves and season with salt and black pepper to taste.

  8. Serve

    Traditionally, Feijoada is served with white rice, farofa, sautéed collard greens (couve), and orange slices.
