Feijao Tropeiro

About:

Feijao tropeiro is a traditional Brazilian dish made with cooked beans, bacon, sausage, collard greens, eggs, and cassava flour.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked carioca/pinto beans, drained.
  • 150g bacon, diced.
  • 1 medium onion, chopped.
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced.
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 cups finely sliced collard greens (couve)
  • 1–1½ cups toasted cassava flour (farinha de mandioca)
  • 2 tablespoons chopped parsley or cilantro
  • 1–2 tablespoons cooking oil, if needed
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Optional: 1–2 links of linguiça calabresa, sliced

  1. Cook the bacon

    Put the bacon in a large skillet over medium heat. Cook until crispy and it has released plenty of fat. If using calabresa, add it and cook until browned.

  2. Sauté the aromatics

    Add the onion and cook for 3–4 minutes until softened. Add the garlic and cook for another 30 seconds.

  3. Add the eggs

    Push everything to one side of the skillet. Crack in the eggs and scramble them. Once cooked, mix them with the bacon and onion.

  4. Add the beans

    Gently fold in the cooked beans. Be careful not to mash them—you want the beans to stay mostly whole.

  5. Add the collard greens

    Stir in the sliced couve and cook for about 2–3 minutes, just until wilted.

  6. Add the farinha

    Lower the heat and gradually sprinkle in the cassava flour while stirring. Add enough to make the mixture pleasantly dry and crumbly, but not so much that it becomes dusty or dry.

  7. Season

    Taste and add salt and black pepper. Finish with parsley or cilantro.
