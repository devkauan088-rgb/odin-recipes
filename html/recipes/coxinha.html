Coxinha

About:

Coxinha (/koʊˈʃiːnjə/ koh-SHEEN-yə; Portuguese: [koˈʃĩɲɐ], little [chicken] thigh) is a Brazilian snack of Paulista origin which consists of chopped or shredded chicken meat, covered in dough, molded into a shape resembling a teardrop, battered and fried.

Ingredients

We have 3 categories for the ingredients,for the filling, for the dough, for breading.

For the filling:

  • 1lb (450g) boneless chicken breast or thighs.
  • 1 small onion, finely choped.
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced.
  • 2 tbsp cream cheese (optional, but common).
  • Salt and black pepper to taste.
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley.

For the dough:

  • 2 cups (475ml) chicken broth (preferably from cooking the chicken).
  • 2 tbsp butter.
  • 2 cups (250g) all-purpose flour.
  • ½ tsp salt

For the breading:

  • 2 eggs, beaten.
  • 1-2 cups breadcrumbs.
  • Vegetable oil for frying.

Instructions

We've some tips, if u want to check it out tips.

  1. Make the filling

    1. Cook the chicken in water or broth until tender (about 20 minutes).
    2. Shred it finely.
    3. Sauté the onion and garlic in a little oil.
    4. Add the shredded chicken, salt, pepper, parsley, and cream cheese if using.
    5. Cook for a few minutes and let cool completely.

  2. Make the dough

    1. Bring the chicken broth, butter and salt to a boil.
    2. Add all the flour at once.
    3. Stir vigorously with a wooden spoon until a smooth dough forms and pulls away from the pan.
    4. Transfer to a surface and knead while still warm until smooth.

  3. Shape the coxinhas

    1. Take a golf-ball-sized piece of dough.
    2. Flattern it into a disk.
    3. Place about 1 tablespoon of filling in the center.
    4. Fold the dough around the filling and shape it into a teardrop (drumstick) shape.

  4. Bread them

    1. Dip each coxinha in beaten egg.
    2. Roll in breadcrumbs until fully coated.

  5. Fry

    1. Heat oil to 350F (175C).
    2. Fry a few at a time fir 4-6 minutes, until golden brown.

    Tips

    • The dough should be warm when shaping: cold dough can crack.
    • Finely shredded chicken makes shaping easier.
    • You can freeze breaded, uncooked coxinhas and fry them straight from frozen, adding a couple of extra minutes to the cooking time.
