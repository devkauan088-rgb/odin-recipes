Pao de Queijo

About:

Pao de queijo cheese bread or Brazilian cheese bread is a small, baked cheese roll or cheese ball, a popular snack and breakfast food in Brazil. It is a traditional Brazilian recipe, originating in the state of Minas Gerais.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups (250 g) tapioca starch (polvilho doce)
  • ½ cup (120 ml) whole milk
  • ¼ cup (60 ml) water
  • ¼ cup (60 ml) vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 cup (100–120 g) grated cheese — traditionally Minas cheese, but mozzarella + Parmesan works well

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. In a saucepan, combine the milk, water, oil, and salt. Bring to a boil.
  3. Put the tapioca starch in a large bowl. Carefully pour the hot liquid over it and mix with a wooden spoon.
  4. Let it cool for a few minutes, then add the egg and mix well.
  5. Add the grated cheese and knead until you have a soft, sticky dough.
  6. Lightly grease your hands and roll the dough into small balls, about the size of a walnut.
  7. Place them on a baking sheet, leaving a little space between each one.
  8. Bake for 18–25 minutes, until puffed and lightly golden.
  9. Eat them warm—that's when they're at their cheesiest!
